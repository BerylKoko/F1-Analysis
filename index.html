01 / THE PEAK

Hamilton’s “normal” was ridiculous.

Mercedes won seven straight Constructors’ Championships from 2014–2020. Hamilton started 137 Grands Prix in that stretch. He won 73, finished on the podium 111 times, and took 67 poles.

02 / WHAT CHANGED?

Mercedes stopped winning everything.

Mercedes still took the 2021 Constructors’ title. After the 2022 regulation reset, the team finished third, second and fourth across 2022–2024. Hamilton’s results dropped with it: zero wins in 2022, zero in 2023, then two in 2024.

That does not make the car a complete explanation. It does make raw win totals a bad way to measure the driver by themselves.

04 / FERRARI

2025 was brutal. Then 2026 happened.

Hamilton’s first Ferrari season ended without a Grand Prix podium: sixth in the championship, 156 points, and behind Leclerc in both qualifying and comparable races. Ferrari also fell from second in the 2024 Constructors’ standings to fourth in 2025.

By September 2026, the picture is different: a win, five podiums, third in the championship, an even qualifying head-to-head, and a race head-to-head lead over Leclerc.

SO, IS THE GOAT FADED?

Some of the decline is real.
The simple version isn’t.

01

The peak did end.

His win, podium and pole rates are nowhere near the 2014–2020 level.

02

The car fell too.

Mercedes moved away from title-winning machinery at the same time Hamilton’s headline results dropped.

03

2024–25 matter.

Those teammate comparisons, especially qualifying, are real evidence of weaker relative performance.

04

But 2026 matters too.

The rebound against Leclerc makes a straight year-by-year fade hard to defend.

My read: Hamilton is no longer the driver who turned half a season into wins. But judging every later year against that version of him makes “decline” look cleaner than it is. The data shows a driver past his peak, cars that also moved backwards, one ugly adaptation year, and a 2026 season that refuses to fit the neat ending.

METHOD

How I checked it

Outcome rates
Wins, podiums and poles divided by Grand Prix starts.

Teammate comparison
Qualifying H2H, race H2H and share of teammate-pair championship points.

Car context
Constructor championship position beside Hamilton’s results.

Limits
Teammates are not perfect controls. 2026 is incomplete. H2H rules are documented in the repo.
